## Día 01 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): The Wall Palms Press
* **Subtítulo:** Axial Spine Decompression & Morning Guarding Release
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (3 Sets × 45 Sec) | **Objetivo:** L4–L5 Lumbar & Chest
* **Por qué funciona:** Overnight, fluid settles and your paraspinal muscles tighten into a protective morning shell. Pressing your palms gently into a solid vertical wall transfers upper-body weight away from your compressed L4–L5 lumbar discs while signaling immediate postural safety to your brainstem.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand facing a clear wall, approximately one arm's length away. Place your feet hip-width apart with your weight balanced evenly across your heels and mid-foot.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Place both palms flat against the wall at chest height, fingers spread softly like roots.
  1. Keep a micro-softness behind both knees—never lock your leg joints.
  1. Take a half-step back and allow your chest to melt gently downward between your upper arms while your hands stay anchored.
  1. Feel a long, soothing traction from your tailbone all the way up to the base of your neck.
* **Respiración:** Inhale quietly through your nose for 4 seconds, expanding your side ribs. Exhale through softly parted lips for 6 seconds, letting your breastbone sink 1 centimeter closer to the floor.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Day 1 is not about stretching hard—it is about introducing your nervous system to the feeling of being supported. Let the wall hold you."*

## Día 02 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): The Scapular Wall Melt
* **Subtítulo:** Upper Thoracic Unwinding & Shoulder-Blade Glide
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (3 Sets × 40 Sec) | **Objetivo:** Thoracic Spine & Traps
* **Por qué funciona:** When your upper back (thoracic spine) is stiff from pillows, reading, or looking at a phone, your lower back is forced to overwork. Freeing the shoulder blades against the wall immediately relieves downward pressure on your lumbar vertebrae.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand facing the wall, slightly closer than arm's length. Place your forearms vertically against the wall from elbows to palms at shoulder height.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Anchor your forearms lightly on the wall, parallel to each other like train tracks.
  1. Step your feet back 6 inches and let your forehead rest gently on the wall between your forearms.
  1. Without moving your elbows, allow your chest to gently sink forward toward the wall for 3 seconds, letting your shoulder blades glide softly together.
  1. Then press lightly through your forearms to round and widen the space between your shoulder blades.
* **Respiración:** Inhale for 4 seconds as you widen and separate the shoulder blades. Exhale for 6 seconds as your heart melts softly toward the wall.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Notice how much tension you unconsciously carry in your shoulders before your first cup of coffee. Let the wall absorb that weight."*

## Día 03 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): Standing L-Traction Hinge
* **Subtítulo:** Gravitational L4–L5 Disc Rehydration Protocol
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (3 Sets × 45 Sec) | **Objetivo:** L4–L5 & Sacrum
* **Por qué funciona:** Your lumbar intervertebral discs act like tiny sponges. By creating a supported 90-degree hip hinge with your hands anchored on the wall, you remove vertical gravity compression so nutrient-rich fluid can re-enter stiff lower back discs.
* **Posición inicial:** Place both palms flat on the wall at hip-to-navel height. Walk your feet back until your torso is angled comfortably toward a soft 'L' shape.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Keep your knees generously soft (bent about 15 degrees) so your hamstrings never pull on your lower back.
  1. Press your palms lightly forward into the wall while imagining someone gently pulling your hips backward with a silk ribbon.
  1. Keep your neck neutral so your ears align naturally between your upper arms.
  1. Sway your hips 1 inch to the right and 1 inch to the left, feeling each side of your lower back release.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds into your lower back ribs. Exhale 6 seconds as you lengthen your tailbone away from the wall.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Keep those knees soft! A soft knee is the secret key that unlocks a tight lower back."*

## Día 04 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): Wall-Supported Cat-Cow Wave
* **Subtítulo:** Segmental Spinal Articulation Without Floor Kneeling
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (2 Sets × 8 Slow Waves) | **Objetivo:** Full Spine Articulation
* **Por qué funciona:** Traditional Cat-Cow requires kneeling on hard floors, which crushes sensitive kneecaps. Performing this wave standing at the wall gives you 100% of the synovial spinal lubrication with zero patellar pressure.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand half an arm's length from the wall. Place both palms comfortably on the wall at ribcage height, feet hip-width apart.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Start at your tailbone: gently tuck your pelvis under, rounding your lower back, mid-back, and finally letting your chin nod toward your chest.
  1. Pause for 2 seconds, feeling the gentle stretch along the back of your spine.
  1. Reverse slowly from the pelvis: tip your tailbone slightly back, let your belly soften, and lift your sternum and gaze softly toward the wall.
  1. Move vertebra by vertebra like a slow ocean wave rather than a stiff hinge.
* **Respiración:** Inhale for 4 seconds as your chest opens toward the wall. Exhale for 6 seconds as you round your spine and tuck your tailbone.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Imagine your spine is a string of pearls lifting and laying down one pearl at a time."*

## Día 05 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): Lateral Ribcage & QL Arc
* **Subtítulo:** Side-Waist & Quadratus Lumborum Decompression
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (45 Sec Per Side × 2) | **Objetivo:** QL Muscle & Side Ribs
* **Por qué funciona:** 80% of 'mysterious' one-sided lower back pain actually comes from a locked Quadratus Lumborum (QL)—the deep muscle connecting your lowest rib to your pelvis. Opening the side body at the wall releases this vise-grip immediately.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand sideways to the wall, about 12 inches away. Place your inside hand or forearm on the wall at shoulder height.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Keep both feet planted hip-width apart with soft knees.
  1. Sweep your outside arm up and gently curve your torso toward the wall while letting your outer hip drift 2 inches away from the wall.
  1. Create a soft, crescent-moon arc along the outside of your waist and ribcage.
  1. Hold comfortably and breathe directly into the open side of your ribs, then turn around and repeat on the other side.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds to expand the outer ribs like an accordion. Exhale 6 seconds to soften the waist.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"When you breathe into your side ribs, your diaphragm massages the top of your lower back from the inside out."*

## Día 06 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): Thoracic Open-Book at the Wall
* **Subtítulo:** Gentle Rotational Unwinding for Mid-Back & Ribs
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (6 Slow Opens Per Side) | **Objetivo:** Mid-Back & Rib Mobility
* **Por qué funciona:** Stiff ribs and mid-back vertebrae force your lower lumbar spine to twist every time you reach into the back seat of a car or turn in kitchen cabinets. Restoring thoracic rotation protects your L4–L5 discs all day long.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand facing the wall, one arm's length away. Place your left palm flat in the center of the wall at shoulder height.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Keep your hips and knees facing the wall (imagine headlights on your hip bones shining straight ahead).
  1. Inhale as you sweep your right arm open to the right side, following your hand softly with your eyes.
  1. Pause for 3 seconds in your pain-free open position, feeling your chest and mid-back gently rotate.
  1. Exhale as you float your right hand back to meet the wall. Complete 6 slow reps, then switch arms.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds as you open your arm wide. Exhale 6 seconds as you return smoothly to the wall.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Let your eyes lead the movement slowly—where your gaze goes, your spine follows with ease."*

## Día 07 (Week I · Spinal & Lumbar Decompression): The Vagal Breath & Spine Flow
* **Subtítulo:** Week 1 Milestone Integration & Nervous System Reset
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (Continuous 5-Min Flow) | **Objetivo:** Vagus Nerve & Full Spine
* **Por qué funciona:** You have completed your first 6 days of mechanical decompression. Day 7 cements this new spinal length into your autonomic nervous system by pairing gentle wall support with vagal toning breathwork.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand close to the wall. Stack your palms or forearms on the wall at forehead height and rest your forehead gently against the back of your hands.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Minute 1–2: Perform 6 slow Wall Palms Presses & Cat-Cow Waves to warm the spine.
  1. Minute 3–5: Rest your forehead on your stacked hands at the wall. Soften your knees, jaw, and belly.
  1. Perform 10 slow 4–6 breath cycles, feeling your entire spine hang Effortlessly from the base of your skull.
  1. Notice how much taller and lighter your lower back feels compared to Day 1.
* **Respiración:** 4-second nasal inhale into the back of your ribs; 6-second audible sigh exhale through the mouth.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Congratulations on completing Week 1! Your spine now knows the wall is a place of relief, not strain."*

## Día 08 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): Supported Standing Hip Circles
* **Subtítulo:** Synovial Fluid Lubrication for Clicking or Stiff Hip Sockets
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (45 Sec Per Side × 2) | **Objetivo:** Hip Acetabulum (Socket)
* **Por qué funciona:** In Somatic Mobility, we say 'motion is lotion.' The ball-and-socket joint of your hip has no direct blood supply inside the cartilage—it relies on gentle, unloaded circular motion to pump fresh synovial fluid across dry, clicking joint surfaces.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand sideways to the wall. Rest your inside palm flat on the wall at chest height for 100% rock-solid balance.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Shift your weight onto your inside standing leg, keeping that knee micro-soft.
  1. Lift your outside foot just 2 inches off the floor so the hip joint hangs freely.
  1. Imagine a paintbrush attached to your kneecap: draw 5 slow, coin-sized circles clockwise in the air.
  1. Pause, then draw 5 slow circles counter-clockwise. Turn around and repeat on the other leg.
* **Respiración:** Breathe continuously and smoothly (4s in, 6s out)—never hold your breath while balancing.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Smaller is smarter! A tiny, buttery-smooth circle lubricates the joint 3x better than a big, clunky circle."*

## Día 09 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): The Wall Pelvic Clock (12-to-6)
* **Subtítulo:** Sacroiliac (SI) Joint Reset & Lumbar-Pelvic Rhythm
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (10 Slow Tilts × 2 Sets) | **Objetivo:** Pelvis, Sacrum & SI Joint
* **Por qué funciona:** Hours of sitting or standing locks your pelvis into an anterior tilt, jamming the sacroiliac (SI) joints at the base of your spine. Using the flat surface of the wall as biofeedback teaches your pelvis to glide smoothly back into neutral alignment.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand with your back to the wall. Rest your shoulder blades and buttocks lightly against the wall, heels walked 4 to 5 inches forward.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Notice the natural small tunnel of space between your lower back and the wall.
  1. Exhale as you gently tip your pubic bone upward (toward 12 o'clock), imprinting your lower back softly flat against the wall.
  1. Hold this supported imprint for 3 seconds—feel your lower back muscles lengthen.
  1. Inhale as you slowly release your pelvis back to neutral (6 o'clock) without over-arching.
* **Respiración:** Exhale for 6 seconds as you flatten your lumbar spine into the wall; inhale for 4 seconds to release.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"The wall never lies—it gives your brain instant tactile feedback of where neutral posture truly lives."*

## Día 10 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): Zero-Kneel Standing Psoas Release
* **Subtítulo:** Deep Hip Flexor Unwinding Without Touching the Floor
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (45 Sec Per Side × 2) | **Objetivo:** Iliopsoas & Front Hip
* **Por qué funciona:** Your psoas muscle attaches directly to your lumbar vertebrae (L1–L5) and crosses the front of your hip. When tight from sitting, it yanks your lower back forward every time you stand up. This standing wall variation unlocks the psoas without ever kneeling on a floor lunge.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand facing the wall, hands resting at shoulder height. Step your right foot back into a comfortable, short staggered stance.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Keep your back heel softly anchored on the floor (or slightly lifted if your calf is tight).
  1. Here is the magic key: before leaning forward, gently tuck your tailbone under (posterior pelvic tilt).
  1. While keeping that subtle tailbone tuck, bend your front knee 2 inches toward the wall.
  1. Stop the exact moment you feel a warm, vertical lengthening down the front of your right hip crease.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds into your lower belly; exhale 6 seconds and imagine your psoas melting like warm wax.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Remember: the psoas is an emotional muscle connected to your fight-or-flight response. Coax it open gently—never force it."*

## Día 11 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): Outer Hip & Sciatic Pathway Glide
* **Subtítulo:** Glute Medius & Piriformis Decompression Against the Wall
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (45 Sec Per Side × 2) | **Objetivo:** Piriformis & Outer Hip
* **Por qué funciona:** Deep ache in the side of the hip or radiating tension down the outer leg often comes from a tight piriformis pressing near the sciatic pathway. Gently leaning into the wall releases the lateral hip capsule safely.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand sideways to the wall, about 10 inches away. Place your inside forearm flat against the wall.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Cross your outside ankle loosely in front of your inside ankle so both feet rest flat on the floor.
  1. Keep your torso upright and gently let your inside hip lean 2 inches toward the wall.
  1. Then slowly shift your hip back to center. Repeat this slow, rhythmic hip-glide 8 times.
  1. On the 8th rep, hold the gentle lean for 3 slow breaths, then switch sides.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds at center; exhale 6 seconds as you let your hip soften toward the wall.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"If you ever feel morning hip bursitis tenderness or sciatic tightness, this gentle glide is your best friend."*

## Día 12 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): Patella Offload & Ankle Glide
* **Subtítulo:** Zero-Torque Knee Lubrication & Calf Fascia Release
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (10 Slow Glides Per Leg) | **Objetivo:** Knee Joint & Ankle
* **Por qué funciona:** When ankles are stiff, your knees are forced to absorb rotational shock with every step you take. By offloading 70% of your bodyweight into your hands on the wall, you can restore ankle and knee glide without grinding the kneecap.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand facing the wall, hands flat at chest height. Step one foot 8 inches forward and the other 8 inches back.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Transfer generous weight into your palms on the wall so your legs feel light and unloaded.
  1. Keep your front heel glued to the floor and slowly glide your front knee 2 inches forward over your middle toe.
  1. Hold for 2 seconds, feeling the ankle hinge and back calf lengthen while the kneecap tracks smoothly.
  1. Glide smoothly back. Complete 10 slow glides on the right leg, then 10 on the left.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds as the knee glides forward; exhale 6 seconds as you press back to neutral.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Healthy, pain-free knees start with mobile ankles and hips. Your knees are caught in the middle—today we free them."*

## Día 13 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): Supported Standing Figure-4 Unwind
* **Subtítulo:** External Hip Rotator Release Using Wall Stability
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (40 Sec Per Side × 2) | **Objetivo:** Deep Rotators & Glutes
* **Por qué funciona:** Getting down on the floor for a 'Pigeon pose' is impossible or painful for most women over 45. Using the wall as a vertical backrest lets you open the deep external rotators of the hip while standing safely.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand with your back leaning comfortably against the wall, feet walked 10 inches forward, hip-width apart.
* **Pasos:**
  1. With your back fully supported by the wall, cross your right ankle gently over your left shin (or just above the left ankle if hips are tight).
  1. Allow your right knee to externally rotate open to the side.
  1. Keep your spine resting flat against the wall—let the wall hold 100% of your balance.
  1. Optionally bend your standing left knee 1 to 2 inches down the wall until you feel a gentle outer-hip release.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds; exhale 6 seconds as you let the crossed knee soften open without pushing it.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Never force the hip open. When your back is supported by the wall, the hip socket opens on its own."*

## Día 14 (Week II · Hip Lubrication, Psoas & Knee Relief): Week 2 Lower-Body Fluidity Flow
* **Subtítulo:** Seamless 5-Minute Hip, Pelvis & Knee Integration
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (Continuous 5-Min Sequence) | **Objetivo:** Hips, Pelvis & Knees
* **Por qué funciona:** Day 14 links your newly lubricated hip sockets, unlocked psoas, and aligned pelvis into a single fluid sequence. Moving smoothly from one position to the next trains your joints to stay mobile when walking and climbing stairs.
* **Posición inicial:** Begin standing sideways to your wall with one hand resting lightly at chest height.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Minute 1: Supported Standing Hip Circles (30 seconds right leg, 30 seconds left leg).
  1. Minute 2: Turn your back to the wall for 8 slow Wall Pelvic Clock Tilts (12-to-6).
  1. Minute 3–4: Face the wall for the Zero-Kneel Standing Psoas Release (45 seconds each side).
  1. Minute 5: Finish with 6 slow Patella Offload & Ankle Glides per leg.
* **Respiración:** Maintain a steady, unhurried 4-second nasal inhale and 6-second mouth exhale throughout all transitions.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Two full weeks complete! Notice how getting out of bed and rising from chairs already feels different."*

## Día 15 (Week III · Full-Chain Integration & Longevity Habit): The Supported Wall Angels
* **Subtítulo:** Postural Re-Alignment for Rounded Shoulders & Upper Spine
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (2 Sets × 8 Slow Glides) | **Objetivo:** Upper Back, Neck & Chest
* **Por qué funciona:** Gravity slowly pulls our head and shoulders forward over the decades, creating a 'dowager's hump' and compressing the entire spine downward. Wall Angels reverse forward-head posture and open the pectoral fascia in minutes.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand with your back against the wall, feet 5 inches forward. Gently imprint your mid-back and ribs toward the wall.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Raise your arms to the sides in a soft 'W' or 'Cactus' shape, elbows bent at 90 degrees.
  1. Rest your elbows and the backs of your wrists toward the wall (it is completely normal if they don't touch the wall yet!).
  1. Inhale as you slowly glide your arms 3 to 5 inches upward along the wall.
  1. Exhale as you draw your elbows smoothly back down into your side waist, feeling your lower shoulder blades engage.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds as arms float up; exhale 6 seconds as elbows glide softly down.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Never sacrifice your lower back just to touch your wrists to the wall. Prioritize a flat, calm ribcage."*

## Día 16 (Week III · Full-Chain Integration & Longevity Habit): Suboccipital & Jaw Tension Release
* **Subtítulo:** Cranial-Cervical Alignment & Neck Decompression
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (2 Sets × 8 Micro-Nods) | **Objetivo:** Cervical Spine & Jaw
* **Por qué funciona:** For every inch your head drifts forward, it adds 10 extra pounds of strain on your neck and lower back. Resetting the tiny suboccipital muscles at the base of your skull relieves tension headaches, neck stiffness, and jaw clenching.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand with your back and the back of your head resting lightly against the wall (place a small folded towel behind your head if needed).
* **Pasos:**
  1. Keep the back of your head in light contact with the wall.
  1. Perform a tiny 'Yes' micro-nod: gently lengthen the back of your neck up the wall while your chin dips 1 centimeter inward.
  1. Hold for 3 seconds—keep your teeth slightly parted and your tongue resting softly on the roof of your mouth.
  1. Slowly release back to neutral. Repeat 8 gentle times.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds to prepare; exhale 6 seconds as the back of your neck lengthens upward.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Your jaw and your pelvis are neurologically linked. Soften your jaw, and your lower back softens too."*

## Día 17 (Week III · Full-Chain Integration & Longevity Habit): Standing Posterior Fascia Unwind
* **Subtítulo:** Hamstring, Calf & Plantar Fascia Release Without Bending Over
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (45 Sec Per Leg × 2) | **Objetivo:** Hamstrings & Back Line
* **Por qué funciona:** Touching your toes from a standing position puts dangerous shear force on aging lumbar discs. By anchoring your hands on the wall and alternating gentle heel-pedals, you release the entire superficial back line safely.
* **Posición inicial:** Face the wall at arm's length. Place both palms flat at chest height and step back into a gentle 60-degree wall hinge.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Keep your spine long and supported by your hands on the wall.
  1. Slowly bend your right knee while letting your left heel sink heavy into the floor.
  1. Hold for 1 full breath, feeling a gentle lengthening behind your left calf and upper hamstring.
  1. Switch smoothly, pedaling left and right at a slow, meditative tempo for 90 seconds.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds as you switch legs; exhale 6 seconds as you sink into the heel.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Your plantar fascia (bottom of the feet), calves, hamstrings, and lower back are one continuous ribbon of tissue."*

## Día 18 (Week III · Full-Chain Integration & Longevity Habit): Cross-Body Somatic Pendulum
* **Subtítulo:** Contralateral Shoulder-to-Hip Neuromuscular Slings
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (8 Slow Swings Per Side) | **Objetivo:** Diagonal Fascial Slings
* **Por qué funciona:** Human walking is a cross-body pattern: your right arm swings with your left leg. Re-coordinating this diagonal fascial sling against the wall restores youthful, effortless grace to your stride.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand sideways to the wall, left hand resting on the wall for balance.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Stand tall on your left leg with a micro-soft knee.
  1. Gently swing your right leg 4 inches forward while your right arm swings softly backward.
  1. Then let your right leg pendulum 4 inches backward while your right arm floats forward.
  1. Let the limb weight swing like a heavy grandfather clock pendulum—zero muscle forcing.
* **Respiración:** Inhale for 2 swings; exhale for 3 swings in a relaxed rhythm.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Youthful movement isn't about strength—it is about rhythm and coordination."*

## Día 19 (Week III · Full-Chain Integration & Longevity Habit): Morning Joint & Lymphatic Flush
* **Subtítulo:** Whole-Body Circulation & Synovial Awakening
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (3 Rounds × 60 Seconds) | **Objetivo:** Lymphatics & All Joints
* **Por qué funciona:** Unlike your heart, your lymphatic system has no pump—it relies on gentle rhythmic calf contraction and deep diaphragmatic breathing to clear morning puffiness and joint inflammation.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand facing the wall, fingertips or palms resting lightly at chest height, feet hip-width apart.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Inhale as you rise softly onto the balls of your feet (lifting heels just 1 to 2 inches).
  1. Exhale as you lower your heels softly to the floor and bend your knees 1 inch in a gentle micro-bounce.
  1. Follow immediately with 4 slow Wall Cat-Cow waves.
  1. Finish with 30 seconds of resting your forehead on your hands at the wall.
* **Respiración:** Inhale 4 seconds as you rise; exhale 6 seconds as your heels kiss the floor.
* **Nota de Elena:** *"Your calves are your 'second heart.' Twenty gentle heel pumps wake up circulation better than espresso."*

## Día 20 (Week III · Full-Chain Integration & Longevity Habit): The Complete 5-Minute Master Flow
* **Subtítulo:** Full-Body Dress Rehearsal Linking Spine, Hips & Posture
* **Dosificación:** 5 Minutes (5 Continuous 1-Min Blocks) | **Objetivo:** Full Somatic Chain
* **Por qué funciona:** Today we weave the five most powerful movements from Weeks 1, 2, and 3 into a single, seamless 5-minute morning ritual that addresses every major joint from your neck to your ankles.
* **Posición inicial:** Stand at your favorite wall space in comfortable morning clothes, barefoot or in non-slip socks.
* **Pasos:**
  1. Minute 1 (Spine): Wall Palms Press into 5 slow Standing Cat-Cow Waves.
  1. Minute 2 (Hips): Supported Standing Hip Circles (30 seconds right, 30 seconds left).
  1. Minute 3 (Psoas): Zero-Kneel Standing Psoas Release (30 seconds right, 30 seconds left).
  1. Minute 4 (Posture): Back to the wall for 6 Pelvic Tilts + 6 Supported Wall Angels.
  1. Minute 5 (Nervous System): Forehead resting on hands at the wall for 6 Vagal 4–6 Breaths.

* **Nota de Elena:** *"Count in breaths, not seconds! Five slow breaths per movement means you never even need a timer."*
